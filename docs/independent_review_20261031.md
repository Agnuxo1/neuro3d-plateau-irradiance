## Verified scientific strengths

1. The CPU reference run covers 1,190 buildings and 44,902 valid computed triangles from Hiroshima CityGML LOD2; the interactive viewer exposes the per-surface computed results.
2. The existing like-for-like CPU comparison is 1,075.59 s (Cycles) against 40.646 s (Neuro3D) for 900 query surfaces: **26.46×**, preprocessing excluded. This is a measured configuration, not a hardware-independent guarantee.
3. On the declared post-hoc non-dark subset of 772 surfaces, R² is **0.98493**, with 2.57% median relative error. This analysis helps distinguish model performance on illuminated geometry from enclosed CityGML overlaps; it does not supersede the full 900-probe verdict.
4. The original analytic self-tests were independently rerun on the checked-out source and reported **FAILURES: none**, with T1–T4 all within the published tolerances.

## New check A — Physical constraints

**Hypothesis:** all 44,902 included surfaces should have finite numerical values, positive area, non-negative direct/diffuse components, physically bounded sky-view factors and shaded irradiance no greater than unshaded irradiance.

**Observed:** 44,902 / 44,902 surfaces satisfy all six checked invariants. No violations.

**Interpretation:** supports numerical integrity of the stored predictions. It does not validate weather-dependent performance against field measurements.

## New check B — Area-based exposure

**Hypothesis:** equal weighting of all tessellated triangles gives a different district summary from weighting each triangle by its area; the distinction matters to planners.

**Observed:** mean annual exposure per triangle **601.6016 kWh/m²**; surface-area-weighted mean **550.2432 kWh/m²**, a **−8.54%** shift. Total included surface area is **2,137,919 m²**. Area-weighted median exposure **292.6938 kWh/m²** and area-weighted mean sky-view factor **0.44078**.

**Interpretation:** aggregate metrics should label whether they are computed per triangle or per square metre. A change in summary weighting is not an improvement or regression in underlying predictions.

## New check C — Stratified bootstrap uncertainty

**Hypothesis:** inferential uncertainty should be reported for the full original 900 Cycles probe comparisons, instead of presenting the point estimate as exact.

**Method:** 1,000 stratified bootstrap draws with replacement, 300 probes per original semantic class; seed **20261031**.

**Observed:** full-set R² **0.8838799** (percentile 95% CI **0.84639–0.91240**); bias **+11.92%** (CI **+9.70% to +14.62%**). Median absolute error **23.4358 kWh/m²·year** (CI **20.2237–27.3834**).

**Interpretation:** the original registered H1 remains **not met**. Resampling existing probes cannot establish independent geographic generalisation or correct spatial dependencies.

## Observations and limitations

The pre-registered full-set accuracy hypothesis H1 is not met (R² = 0.884, bias +11.9%, unstable high relative errors on enclosed near-zero-reference surfaces). The 128 enclosed cases should be addressed by a tested detector update, and any new detector version must be benchmarked on all 900 probes plus a held-out city before promoting revised accuracy claims. Timing excludes preprocessing; Cycles configurations were not optimised for an equal-error benchmark. Photometric predictions use clear-sky assumptions and no measured weather. The new tests are analytical audits on historical outputs, not three independent end-to-end Blender/Cycles experiments.
